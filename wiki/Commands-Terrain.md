# Commands: Terrain

> Generated by `tools/wiki_reference.py`. Voxels, digging, terrain cells, ore veins and the terrain store. Back to [Command Documentation](Command-Documentation).

## Dev commands

Run from `dev_commands.txt` or the admin menu's *Any command* (see [Running dev commands](Command-Documentation#running-dev-commands)). "Console / admins" = the server owner through the file, or an admin.

| Command | Syntax | Who | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `carve` | `carve <dx> <dz> <radius dm> [dy]` | Console / admins | Carve a sphere at the ground next to the first player. |
| `carvefwd` | `carvefwd <ahead m> <right m> <radius dm>` | Console / admins | Carve in front of the player (as they face), for screenshots of holes from the default camera. |
| `cavecheck` | `cavecheck [zone\|all]` | Console / admins | Does every cavern entry point of the landscape have a floor (Caves.CheckFloors), and where do its cavern graveyards' arrivals stand: on the plinth, the client's own floor under them, their depth below the ground, how many random trips may use (Graveyards.CheckReport). all = every landscape with cavern graveyards, in the background. |
| `cavelands` | `cavelands [zone]` | Console / admins | Every landscape with Chaos Caverns and where its outposts stand; with a zone, its outposts are queued for placement now (background, one at a time). |
| `cellstats` | `cellstats [x y z]` | Console / admins | What the stored layers of the cell at the player (or a local point) hold. |
| `clearcell` | `clearcell <hex cell id>` | Console / admins | Drop every override in one terrain cell on the player's landscape. |
| `dig` | `dig [dx dy dz]` | Console / admins | A pick swing at an offset from the player (default 2 m ahead, 1 m down). |
| `lodbuild` | `lodbuild` | Console / admins | Refreshes the coarse copies of the edits on the player's landscape now (Terrain.TerrainLod; normally automatic once edits have been quiet for 20 s). |
| `matname` | `matname <n>` | Console / admins | VoxelMaterials rows with ID n and with ARRAY_INDEX n (name, item). |
| `nearvein` | `nearvein [radius]` | Console / admins | The nearest ore veins (kind, nearest point, distance); "nearvein go" stands the player 2 m from the nearest one. |
| `outpostrotate` | `outpostrotate [zone\|here] [count]` | Console / admins | One rotation of a landscape's outposts now, whatever OutpostRotationHours says: count outposts (default the configured share) get another ruin in their own cavern, in the background; outposts a player is near are skipped. Writes terrain edits and the landscape's outpost marker (back up server-data first). |
| `ownblock` | `ownblock [material] [edge voxels]` | Console / admins | A cube built 3 m east of the player as the player's own voxels (the paste path: overlay + Voxel Vision ownership), sent to everyone in range. |
| `regendelay` | `regendelay <seconds>` | Console / admins | Terrain regeneration delay (default 1200), checked again now. |
| `terrainmem` | `terrainmem` | Console / admins | Edited terrain cells held, how many expanded, compact bytes. |
| `veins` | `veins <radius>` | Console / admins | Shows every vein within the radius (the Holoseeker's overlay); veins 0 clears it. |
| `veinsweep` | `veinsweep` | Console / admins | Moves every vein that touches a build site out of it (sites placed before OreVeins.ClearSite existed, or veins generated before the site rule). |
| `vertexonly` | `vertexonly on\|off` | Console / admins | Keep the vertex-only voxels of client build diffs (round surfaces). |
| `wall` | `wall <dx> <dz> <length m> <height m> <x\|z> [door m] [material]` | Console / admins | A wall of the player's own voxels (the paste path, like ownblock: overlay + ownership, sent to everyone in range), 0.8 m thick, starting at that offset from the player and running along +x or +z from the ground up (each column's generated ground), with a doorway of that width (2.4 m tall) in its middle. Material 0 takes the same wall away again (its voxels cleared: the generated ground shows). For the site-pathing test (story creatures must go through the doorway / around the wall). |
