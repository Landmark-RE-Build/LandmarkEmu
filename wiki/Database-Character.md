# Database: Character (`lmemu_character`)

> Generated by `tools/wiki_reference.py`. Back to [Database Documentation](Database-Documentation).

The characters and everything that travels with them (items, recipes, unlocks, friends, guilds, mail, designs, Player Studio). Shared by every world: one character visits every world, as on live Landmark. A world without a database of its own also keeps its build sites, props and chests here.

Source: `LandmarkEmulator.Database.Character/Migrations/CharacterContextModelSnapshot.cs` and the model classes in `LandmarkEmulator.Database.Character/Model`. Code: [LandmarkEmulator.Database.Character](Project-Database-Character).

Tables marked *world* are kept in the world's own database instead when the world has one (always under
Docker; see [Database: World](Database-World)).
